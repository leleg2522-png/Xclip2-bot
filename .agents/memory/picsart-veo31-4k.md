separate final-resolution preparation step to customers. The verified source
contract uses the Veo T2V gateway with model `veo-3.1-lite-generate-preview`,

or 3840×2160 for `16:9`. Charge Rp2.500 only for a delivered result; failure at
generation, final preparation, or delivery must refund.

not itself claim a native 4K generation. The public 4K product therefore depends
on completing the already-validated final-resolution workflow.

**How to apply:** Preserve the two-ratio wizard, p500 routing, hidden internal
labels, and final 4K preparation before delivery. Never send the native 720p
result as a successful 4K order or mention “export” in customer-facing copy.
