---
name: Video menu organization
description: User-selected organization for the Telegram bot's video model menus.
---

Group the bot's video choices by model family, rather than by use case or a
single flat list.

**Why:** When offered those three approaches, the user explicitly chose
“Nama model (disarankan)”. The approved cleanup preserves existing models,
prices, and generation behavior; it is not approval to retire models or change
their providers.

**How to apply:** Keep future video variants within their model family, with
clear navigation back to the family list and main menu. Treat adding a model
or changing its generation contract separately from reorganizing its menu.
