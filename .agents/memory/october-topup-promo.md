---
name: October 2026 top-up promotion
description: Qualification, model scope and owner-controlled duration for the Rp1,500 generation promotion.
---

Only one paid top-up transaction of at least Rp50,000 made and paid since
the promotion is activated qualifies an account. Old payments, accumulated
smaller top-ups, manual balance additions, refunds and referral credits do
not qualify.

**Why:** The user lowered the mandatory top-up threshold with “50 k saja deh
promo nya”, superseding the earlier Rp200,000 minimum. The earlier rule
“Hanya top-up sejak promo diaktifkan” still applies, as does the single-payment
requirement.

**How to apply:** Qualifying users pay Rp1,500 per generation for Kling 2.1 Pro,
Heygen, PixVerse v6, ordinary MiniMax H3, and Seedance 2 Mini image-to-video.
Keep Uncensored, Video Edit and other models at normal prices.
Refund the actual generation charge, not the regular list price. Activation
must persist across restarts, and a low remaining balance must not remove
qualification once a qualifying top-up has been paid during the campaign.

The promotion has no automatic expiry; it runs until the owner asks to stop.
Preserve the original activation and previously qualified participants rather
than starting a new campaign or requiring another top-up.

**Why:** The user asked to extend the promo, then specified “sampai saya bilang
berenti”, superseding the original 31 October 2026 deadline.

**How to apply:** Do not add a new fixed deadline. Continue accepting qualifying
paid top-ups after the old deadline under the same rules. Stop only when the
owner requests it; never reset the original eligibility start when extending.
